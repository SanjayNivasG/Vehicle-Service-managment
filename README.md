# 🚗 Vehicle Service Management System

A system designed to manage vehicle servicing operations — helping streamline scheduling, tracking, and record-keeping for a vehicle service center.

## 📖 About

The Vehicle Service Management System helps service centers manage day-to-day operations such as recording customer and vehicle details, tracking service requests, and maintaining service history — reducing manual effort and improving efficiency.

## ✨ Features

- 🧾 Register and manage customer & vehicle details
- 🛠️ Log and track vehicle service requests
- 📅 Maintain service history and records
- 💵 Basic billing for completed services
